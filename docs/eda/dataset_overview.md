# Dataset overview (train split only)

Home Credit Default Risk, `application_train.csv`: one row per loan application, one column per fact known at application time, plus the label. This file describes the 60% train split (`data/processed/train.csv`), generated by `src/data/eda.py`.

- Rows: 184,506. Columns: 122 (16 text, 106 numeric).
- Label: `TARGET`, 1 = default / payment difficulty. 14,895 of 184,506 rows (8.07%) are 1.
- Days columns (`DAYS_BIRTH`, `DAYS_EMPLOYED`, ...) are offsets relative to the application date, so negative numbers mean days before it. There is no calendar date anywhere.
- `DAYS_EMPLOYED` equals 365243 (about 1,000 years) in 33,225 rows (18.0%). It is a placeholder value, not a real duration, and is excluded in the years-employed chart.
- `CODE_GENDER` has 2 rows coded `XNA`; `ORGANIZATION_TYPE` has 33,225 rows coded `XNA`.
- Missingness is covered in `missingness_summary.md` and `missingness_crosschecks.md`.

## What the columns are

| Topic | Columns | What it holds | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| ID and label | 2 | Applicant id and the default flag (TARGET: 1 = payment difficulty / default). | `SK_ID_CURR`, `TARGET` |
| Loan terms | 4 | Contract type and the credit, annuity and goods price of the loan applied for. | `NAME_CONTRACT_TYPE`, `AMT_CREDIT`, `AMT_ANNUITY` |
| Client demographics | 8 | Gender, children, family, education, housing and who accompanied the applicant. | `CODE_GENDER`, `CNT_CHILDREN`, `NAME_TYPE_SUITE` |
| Income and employment | 5 | Income amount and type, occupation, employer type, time employed. | `AMT_INCOME_TOTAL`, `NAME_INCOME_TYPE`, `DAYS_EMPLOYED` |
| Assets | 3 | Car and real-estate ownership, car age. | `FLAG_OWN_CAR`, `FLAG_OWN_REALTY`, `OWN_CAR_AGE` |
| Building / housing attributes | 47 | Normalised statistics about the applicant's building, in three flavours (_AVG, _MODE, _MEDI) per attribute: areas, floors, elevators, year built, wall material. | `APARTMENTS_AVG`, `BASEMENTAREA_AVG`, `YEARS_BEGINEXPLUATATION_AVG` |
| External scores | 3 | Three normalised scores from external data sources. | `EXT_SOURCE_1`, `EXT_SOURCE_2`, `EXT_SOURCE_3` |
| Region and address consistency | 9 | Region rating and population, plus flags for whether registered, living and work addresses differ. | `REGION_POPULATION_RELATIVE`, `REGION_RATING_CLIENT`, `REGION_RATING_CLIENT_W_CITY` |
| Contact flags | 6 | Whether mobile, work phone, home phone, email etc. were provided. | `FLAG_MOBIL`, `FLAG_EMP_PHONE`, `FLAG_WORK_PHONE` |
| Document flags | 20 | FLAG_DOCUMENT_2..21: whether each document was provided. | `FLAG_DOCUMENT_2`, `FLAG_DOCUMENT_3`, `FLAG_DOCUMENT_4` |
| Social circle | 4 | Observed and defaulted counts among the applicant's social surroundings (30 / 60 days). | `OBS_30_CNT_SOCIAL_CIRCLE`, `DEF_30_CNT_SOCIAL_CIRCLE`, `OBS_60_CNT_SOCIAL_CIRCLE` |
| Credit bureau enquiries | 6 | Number of enquiries to the credit bureau (hour / day / week / month / quarter / year). | `AMT_REQ_CREDIT_BUREAU_HOUR`, `AMT_REQ_CREDIT_BUREAU_DAY`, `AMT_REQ_CREDIT_BUREAU_WEEK` |
| Application timing and history | 5 | When the application was made and days since registration, ID document and last phone change. | `DAYS_REGISTRATION`, `DAYS_ID_PUBLISH`, `WEEKDAY_APPR_PROCESS_START` |
